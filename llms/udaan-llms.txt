# Udaan

> Udaan is India's largest eB2B trade platform, connecting small businesses — kirana stores, chemists, hotels, restaurants, and offices — with manufacturers, brands, and wholesalers for sourcing across FMCG, pharma, fresh produce, electronics, and general merchandise. Udaan does not publish a public developer API; its buyer and seller experiences are delivered through its web and mobile apps.
